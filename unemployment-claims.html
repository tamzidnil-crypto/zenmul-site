01 / The clock

The response deadline is short, and it is already running.

When a former employee files, the state sends you a notice. Each state sets its own deadline, and in many states the clock starts on the date printed on the notice, not the day you open it.

  • California, Virginia and South Carolina: 10 calendar days.
  • Texas: 14 calendar days from the date the notice is mailed.
  • Your notice shows the exact deadline for your state. That date is the one that counts.
02 / What it costs

A missed or vague response lets the state decide without your side.

If the state does not hear from you, it decides using the claimant's account alone. An approved claim is charged to your account, and your state unemployment tax rate can go up.

  • A layoff usually qualifies for benefits.
  • Someone who quit without good cause may not.
  • Someone fired for misconduct may not.
  • Not every claim should be contested. Some only need an accurate answer.
03 / What you send
  • The claim notice from the state.
  • Dates of hire and separation, and the reason for separation.
  • Any written warnings, policies the employee signed, or a resignation message.
  • Names of anyone who saw the final incident, if there was one.
04 / What you get
  • A clear verdict: worth contesting, respond accurately, or needs more information.
  • A written response with specific facts, dates and the documents behind them.
  • A hearing prep sheet, if the claim goes to a hearing.
  • Usually within 24 to 48 hours of receiving everything, because the deadline is short.

06 / Questions

Common questions

Do you attend the hearing?

No. You attend. You get a prep sheet so you walk in knowing the facts, the dates and the documents to point to.

Do you need my state portal login?

No. You send me the notice and your records by email, and you submit the response yourself.

Are you a lawyer?

No. I prepare the response papers and the prep sheet. If you want legal advice on a claim, speak to an employment lawyer.

Will you tell me if the claim should just be accepted?

Yes. If the claim looks valid, the verdict says so, and the response is written to answer accurately rather than to fight it.

How much does it cost?

Send the notice and I will tell you the cost before any work starts.
