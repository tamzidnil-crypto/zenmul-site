Questions

Answered honestly

I am not technical. Can I actually use this?

Yes. The workflows run in the background. You interact through tools you already use, like email, Telegram and Google Sheets. All technical maintenance is handled, so there is nothing new for you to learn or manage.

What happens if something breaks?

Every workflow includes monitoring and ongoing support as part of the monthly retainer. If something breaks, fixing it is our job. You are not left managing it.

Why a setup fee and a monthly fee?

The setup fee covers design, build and testing of the system. The monthly covers hosting, monitoring, maintenance and changes as your process changes. Without the monthly, an automation quietly rots the first time a tool updates.

Can I cancel?

Yes, monthly, with no exit fee. You keep everything. Systems are built on open tools like n8n and Google Workspace, so nothing is locked to us.

Do you work with businesses outside the UK and US?

Yes. Billing is in USD and clients are worldwide. Everything runs remotely and asynchronously, so time zones are not an issue.
