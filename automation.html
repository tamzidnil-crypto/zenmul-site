03 / What it does not do

It flags. You decide.

The check is there to make sure nothing slips past. Every decision and every submission stays with you.

  • It never logs in to your carrier, retailer or state portals.
  • It never files a dispute or sends a response on its own.
  • It never contacts the other company.
  • It only reads what you put in the folder or forward to it.
