Law Firms

The bottleneck was never the law, it was the chasing

A law firm was losing enquiries to slow replies and burning hours chasing documents. We built a five-workflow suite that handles the admin so solicitors handle the law.

The problem

Billable time lost to unbillable chasing

A solicitor's most valuable hours get eaten by the least valuable work: replying to enquiries, reminding clients of consultations, and endlessly chasing the documents a matter cannot proceed without.

None of it requires legal skill, yet all of it lands on skilled, expensive people, or it does not get done, and enquiries go cold and matters stall.

What we built

One system covering the whole client lifecycle

Every stage of the customer journey gets its own workflow, and each one runs on its own. Nothing waits on a spare moment that never comes.

Instant replyNew enquiries answered by AI in under 60 seconds
RemindersConfirmation, 24h and 2h consultation reminders automated
Document chaseOutstanding documents chased at day 2, 5 and 10 automatically
No-show recoveryMissed consultations met with an AI-written rebook offer
OnboardingNew clients onboarded with a personalised welcome in seconds

The firm's solicitors got their billable hours back, because the chasing now runs itself.
