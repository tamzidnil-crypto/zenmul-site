About

One person, from the first document to the final file.

Zenmul is run by Nazmul Hossen. I check the paperwork other companies send small businesses, prepare the file to get money back, and set up checks so it stops.

01 / Why this work

The other side has a team. Small businesses usually have nobody checking.

Carriers, retailers and state agencies all have people and systems working on their side of every bill, deduction and claim. A small business owner has a busy week and a pile of documents that look routine.

Most of those documents are fine. Some are not, and those only get caught when someone reads them line by line against the dates, the contract and the rules. That is the job.

02 / How I work

Four things that do not change

No handoffs

I review every document myself and build every monthly check myself. You deal with one person, by email.
