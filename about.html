About

One person, from the first bill to the final file.

Zenmul is run by Nazmul Hossen. I check the freight bills small US importers get from carriers, truckers and forwarders, prepare the file to get overcharges back, and keep watch so the same charges stop coming back.

01 / Why this work

The carrier has a billing team. Small importers usually have nobody checking.

Ocean carriers, terminals and freight companies have people and systems working on their side of every invoice. A small importer has a busy week, a container on the water, and a pile of bills that look routine.

Most of those bills are fine. Some are not, and those only get caught when someone reads them line by line against the container dates, the contract and the rules. Each bill also has a dispute deadline, and once it passes, a wrong charge becomes a paid charge. Catching both is the job.
