01 / The leak

The money leaves quietly, one document at a time.

When you run a small business, the paperwork other companies send you usually gets paid or filed without a second look. On their own, none of these look like much. Added up over a year, they are real money leaving the business without anyone deciding to let it go.

  • A carrier bills detention for days the container could not move.
  • A retailer takes a deduction before anyone proves it is valid.
  • A former employee files an unemployment claim, and the response deadline passes in a busy week.

06 / Questions

Common questions

Do you need my logins?

No. You send me the documents by email. I never log in to your carrier, retailer or state portal.

Will you contact the carrier, retailer or state for me?

No. I prepare the file and you submit it yourself. Nothing goes out in your name without you sending it.

What do I need to send?

For freight: the invoices, plus your gate dates and contract terms if you have them. For deductions: your deduction report, plus POs, invoices, bills of lading and proof of delivery. For claims: the state notice and your records on the separation. Each service page lists it in full.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many documents there are. Send me one and I will tell you what the review would cost before any work starts.

What if there is nothing to recover?

Then I tell you that. A charge that is correct gets marked as correct.

Are you a lawyer or an accountant?

No. I review documents and prepare files. I do not give legal or tax advice, and I do not attend hearings.
