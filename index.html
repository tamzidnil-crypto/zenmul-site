07 / Questions

Common questions

Do I need any technical knowledge?

None. You describe what you want to stop doing by hand. Design, build, testing and maintenance are handled. You use the result.

How long until it is live?

Most systems go live within 5 to 10 business days of the audit. Larger multi workflow systems take longer, and you get the timeline in writing before anything starts.

What tools do you build on?

Primarily n8n, with AI handling the language tasks, connected to Gmail, Google Sheets, Telegram and whatever else your business already uses. No new software for your team to learn.

What happens if it breaks?

Monitoring and maintenance are included in every ongoing plan. If something breaks or your process changes, fixing it is our job, not yours.

What if my industry is not listed?

Book the audit anyway. Slow responses, missed follow ups and manual data entry exist in every service business. If it can be solved you will hear exactly how, and if it cannot you will hear that too.
