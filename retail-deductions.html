01 / Where it goes wrong

The retailer deducts first. Proving it wrong is up to you.

A deduction comes out of your payment before anyone checks it. Not every deduction is valid, and the ones that are not only come back if someone disputes them with proof.

  • A shortage on a delivery that was signed for in full.
  • A price cut taken after a cost increase was approved.
  • An allowance taken above the agreed rate.
  • A compliance fine your shipping records do not support.
02 / The windows

Every deduction has a dispute window, and they close.

Once a window closes, that money is gone, valid or not. The windows below come from published supplier guides, not from Walmart or Target directly, so always check the date shown in your portal for each deduction.

  • Walmart shortage deductions: generally 12 months in APDP.
  • Most other Walmart AP deductions: generally 24 months.
  • Target compliance chargebacks: about 3 months on domestic orders and 6 months on imports.
  • Most other Target deductions: up to 18 months in Synergy.
03 / What you send
  • Your deduction report, exported from APDP or Synergy.
  • The purchase orders and invoices behind each deduction.
  • Bills of lading and signed proof of delivery.
  • Cost change approvals or allowance agreements, if a deduction involves price or allowances.
04 / What you get
  • Every deduction marked invalid, valid, partial or missing proof.
  • Dispute files for the invalid ones, ready for APDP, Synergy or your buyer.
  • An honest note on the valid ones, so you do not waste a dispute.
