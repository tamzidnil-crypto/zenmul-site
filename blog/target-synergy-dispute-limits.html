Target Synergy and the limit.

The windows, the compliance exemption, and the limit on how many times one deduction can be disputed.

Target suppliers dispute most deductions in Synergy, an app inside Partners Online. Many of Target's windows are fairly long, but there is a firm limit on how many times you can dispute the same deduction, so each try needs to count.

Common codes

Target uses roughly 250 deduction codes, though most suppliers only ever see a couple of dozen. A few of the most common:

  • A030: carton shortage, fewer cartons received than invoiced
  • A034: unit shortage
  • A036: cost difference

Codes are updated and retired over time. The description in your Partners Online deduction detail is the one to work from.

The windows

  • Accounts payable deductions: Target will not research claims more than 18 months from the document date shown on the check remittance.
  • Collect shipments: dispute within 9 months of the shipping date, which matches Target's own window to claim against its carriers.
  • Compliance chargebacks: after the exemption window below, supplier guides report about 3 months to dispute in Synergy. The Compliance Research Report shows the expiration date for each one.

The two week compliance window

When Target flags a compliance violation, suppliers have about two weeks to request an exemption before it becomes a chargeback. After that, the only way to challenge it is a dispute in Synergy. Watching for new violations in Partners Online is the cheapest way to stop a fine.

The dispute limit

Supplier guides report that the same deduction can be disputed up to three times, with a fourth attempt as a last effort, and that Target has said it may start issuing fines from the fifth. That makes the first dispute the one that matters most. A rushed dispute with weak proof uses up a try.

How to make each try count

  • One document number per case. Synergy accepts only one per dispute.
  • Research unpaid invoices in Partners Online before opening a dispute.
  • Do not dispute with the chargeback document alone. Attach the shipping documents that prove your side.
  • If Target asks for more documents, send them within 5 business days, or the dispute can be denied.
  • Write a short, specific explanation of why the deduction is wrong.
