Lead Generation

A lead engine that runs itself

SalDev needed a steady flow of qualified B2B leads without a person spending hours a day building the list. We built a pipeline that scrapes, verifies, and personalizes every morning, with zero human touch.

SalDev

The problem

Getting good leads is hours of invisible work

SalDev needed a steady flow of qualified B2B leads, but the way most teams get them does not scale. Someone has to trawl job boards, guess who the decision maker is, hunt down an email, check whether it even works, write a personalized message, and log all of it somewhere useful.

Done by hand, that is hours of repetitive work every single day. And the moment the person doing it gets busy, the pipeline goes quiet. The goal was simple to state and hard to build: a lead engine that runs on its own, produces leads that are actually reachable, and hands over outreach copy that does not read like a template.

What we built

Four stages, running every morning

A fully automated pipeline in n8n. It pulls hiring signals from multiple startup job boards, because a company hiring engineers is a company that is growing, and a growing company is one that can use help scaling. Every stage filters for quality, so what lands at the end is not a pile of scraped rows. It is a short list of real people with a reason to talk.

STAGE 01

Source

Scrapes fresh hiring activity from several startup job boards in parallel, capturing companies actively growing their teams.

STAGE 02

Find & verify

Identifies the decision maker, reveals their email, then independently verifies it so only deliverable addresses survive.

STAGE 03

Personalize

An AI step writes a tailored email for each verified lead, anchored to that company's actual hiring signal, plus a ready follow-up.

STAGE 04

Deliver

Everything is logged to one clean sheet, and a summary is posted to the team's channel. The day's leads are ready before anyone logs on.

Under the hood

The chain, step by step

Each link in the pipeline does one job and hands off cleanly to the next. Together they turn raw listings into a list a founder can actually work.

1
Scrape hiring signalsMultiple startup job boards are scraped in parallel every morning, capturing companies growing their engineering teams.
2
Filter to the right rolesListings are narrowed to genuine engineering roles, removing noise before any enrichment cost is spent.
3
Find the decision makerFor each company, the system identifies the CEO, CTO, founder, or engineering lead, not a generic inbox.
4
Reveal & verify the emailEach email is revealed, then run through an independent verification step, so bouncing addresses never reach the list.
5
Deduplicate & cleanDuplicate contacts from overlapping boards are removed and messy company names are normalized automatically.

7
Log & alertEvery lead is written to a clean sheet and a daily summary is posted to the team's channel. Nothing to check manually.
Quality at every step

From raw noise to reachable leads

The pipeline does not just collect. It narrows. Each stage strips out what will not convert, so the final list is short, verified, and worth a founder's time.

Verified & personalized

In testing, the pipeline consistently takes a raw batch of scraped listings and narrows it, through role filtering, decision-maker matching, deduplication, and email verification, down to a tight set of verified, personalized, ready-to-contact leads each run. The design scales cleanly: adding more job boards or raising enrichment limits lifts daily volume without changing the workflow.

The result

Lead generation on autopilot

The manual work that used to eat into every day, the searching, the verifying, the copy writing, now happens on its own before the team logs on. Instead of building the list, SalDev spends time on the part that matters: following up with qualified prospects who are already reachable and already have a personalized message waiting.

Hours back, daily

The list-building, verifying, and drafting that used to be manual now runs automatically before work starts.

Only reachable leads

Independent email verification means the list is not padded with addresses that bounce.

They own it

The system runs on SalDev's own tools and accounts, so they are never dependent on us to keep it going.

Most "lead generation" is really just scraping, and scraped lists are mostly noise. The value here is everything that happens after.

The finding, verifying, cleaning, and personalizing is what turns raw data into a list a founder can actually work. That is the difference between busywork and a pipeline. If your team is doing this by hand, or paying for lists that do not convert, this is the kind of system that gives you the hours back and the pipeline both.
