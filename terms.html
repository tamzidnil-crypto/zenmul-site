Service Terms

Last updated: November 22, 2026

01

What the service covers

Zenmul checks the per diem, detention and demurrage bills your company receives and tells you which charges do not hold up, and why.

  • I read each bill you send and check it against the federal billing rules (46 CFR Part 541 and Part 545) and your own records: gate times, terminal notices, appointment attempts and emails.
  • For each charge I give a grade: Strong, Needs proof, or Weak.
  • For each charge worth disputing I write a dispute message you can send to the carrier.
  • You get a short summary: what was checked, what can be disputed, and the total amount at stake.
02

Pricing

The first review starts from $149 and covers one month of bills, up to 10 containers. The exact price is agreed before any work starts.

After the first review, you can choose a monthly plan based on your number of trucks.

You can stop a monthly plan at any time. It ends at the close of the month you already paid for.

03

Payment

Payment is made upfront, before the work starts.

  • I send you a payment request by email through Payoneer. You can pay by card or bank transfer there.
  • Monthly plans are paid at the start of each month.
  • Refunds: if I cannot start your review after you pay, you get a full refund. Once the review has been delivered, the fee is not refunded, because the work is done whether or not the carrier agrees to remove the charges.
04

Who does what

You stay in control of every message to the carrier.

  • You send me the bills and any records you have. No login to your systems is needed.
  • I write the disputes. You decide which ones to send, and you send them yourself.
  • Nothing goes to the carrier, terminal or chassis provider from me.
  • If the carrier replies, you can forward the reply and I will help you answer it within your plan.
05

Limits

This is a bill checking service, not legal advice. I am not a lawyer.

  • I cannot promise that a carrier will remove or reduce any charge. The carrier makes that decision.
  • My review is based on the documents you send. If a record is missing or wrong, the result can change.
  • For a lawsuit, a formal FMC complaint or any legal question, please speak with a lawyer.
06

Privacy and your documents

Your documents are used only for your review and are deleted 60 days after the work is finished. See the Privacy policy for details.

  • Please black out anything private before you send a bill, such as bank details or customer names you do not want to share.
  • I do not share your documents or results with anyone else.
  • If you want your files deleted sooner, tell me and I will delete them and confirm by email.
07

How to agree

To accept these terms, reply to my email with "I agree".

After that I will send the Payoneer payment request, and the review starts once payment arrives.
